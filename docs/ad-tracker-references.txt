5. HOW QUIETNET USES THESE TODAY
--------------------------------

- At startup the engine downloads the HaGeZi lists and DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar, refreshes them
  daily, and checks every domain a device looks up against them.
- Gemini gets the list hits and the Tracker Radar owner/category as clues (only the domain name,
  never which device asked).
- Confidence is decided by rules, not by Gemini: on 2+ lists, or confirmed by Tracker Radar, or on
  a threat feed = HIGH; on 1 list = MEDIUM; nothing = LOW.
- Malware and phishing are always blocked straight away.
- Ads and trackers at HIGH confidence are blocked straight away when Auto-mode is on (Settings);
  otherwise they become suggestion cards. The never-block list above always wins.
- Adding more lists from part 1 (OISD, StevenBlack, Perflyst) means more domains reach HIGH, so
  Auto-mode blocks more of them without asking.

Before shipping any list inside the product, check that list's license.
